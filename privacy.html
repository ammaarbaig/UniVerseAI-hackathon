Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

UniVerse is a free tool for students and parents navigating Rajasthan DTE engineering and polytechnic admissions. This page explains, plainly, what happens to the information you type into it.

What we collect

  • The messages you type into the chat with Uni AI.
  • The rank, category and branch you enter into the college predictor.

That's it. We don't ask for anything else to use the site.

Where it goes

Right now, both the chat and the predictor run entirely in your browser — your rank, category and branch are matched against the on-page college data on your own device, and nothing is sent anywhere. When the chat is connected to its external assistant service, the text of your messages is sent to that service so it can generate a reply. No other data — your rank, your predictor inputs, or anything else on the page — is sent along with it.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No payment details — the site is free.
  • No name, phone number or address is required to use the site.

Storage

UniVerse keeps no server-side record of your chat conversation. Closing the tab or refreshing the page clears it. Nothing is saved for next time.

Cookies and analytics

UniVerse does not set cookies and does not run any analytics, tracking or advertising scripts. The site is static pages, styles and scripts — nothing calls home to measure your visit. The page does load its typefaces from Google Fonts' servers, which is a standard web-font request and not something we use to track you.

Students and children

Most people using UniVerse are between 17 and 19, applying straight out of school. Please don't type identifying personal details — your full name, phone number, home address, or similar — into the chat. You don't need to; a rank, category and branch are enough to get a useful answer.

Third-party links

UniVerse links out to official government and counselling portals for further reading and verification. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.
